Frequently asked questions

How do I delete an item?
Swipe left on an item to remove it from your list.
How do I turn an item into a heading?
Swipe right on an item to turn it into a heading.
Can I undo an action?
Yes. You can undo your most recent action.
Does NISHIKI LIST support Markdown?
Yes. You can copy and paste lists in Markdown format.
Why are there no checkboxes?
NISHIKI LIST is designed around a simple idea: write what you need, and remove it when you're done. No extra steps, no unnecessary complexity.
