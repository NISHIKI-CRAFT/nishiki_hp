Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NISHIKI LIST handles information you create and use in the app.

1. Information stored by the app

NISHIKI LIST handles the list names, headings, and item text you enter, along with input history used to suggest previous entries. This history may include the text, how often it was used, and when it was last used.

In the current version, this information is stored on your device. The app does not send it to servers operated by NISHIKI CRAFT.

2. How the information is used

Stored information is used to display, edit, and restore your lists, and to suggest items you have entered before.

3. Managing input history

Input history is stored separately for each list. Removing an item from a list does not automatically remove its history. You can delete individual entries from the suggestions screen.

4. On-device storage and deletion

App data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Data included in device or operating system backups may be handled according to your backup settings and the relevant service's policies.

5. Clipboard and sharing

When you copy text, the selected text is placed on your device's clipboard. When you share text, it is passed to the standard iOS sharing feature. Any further handling is governed by the privacy policy of the service you choose.

6. Sharing with third parties

In the current version, the app does not send your information to servers operated by NISHIKI CRAFT or to third parties for advertising or analytics. The operating system and any sharing services you choose handle information according to their providers' policies.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in applicable laws or in the app. Updates will be posted on this page.

8. Operator and contact

Operated by NISHIKI CRAFT.
